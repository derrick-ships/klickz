## B. Char vs Petro (13 Nov 2026): one contrast, three angles
Main title: "Petro dejó perder los Panamericanos; Char le RESPONDE con un estadio"

| Variant | Face | Expression | Text | Lever | Object | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Recomendado | Char | proud half-smile | OBRAS, / NO ODIO | DESEO | stadium at night with floodlights | The title already says the facts; the thumbnail adds the moral verdict. No ¿?, so "No red text". |
| Alt 1 "La VERDAD de cómo Petro dejó perder…" | Petro | evasive, looking away | ¿QUIÉN NO / PAGÓ? | INTRIGA | unlit torch cauldron, empty stadium | Opens the question the title only hints at (the payment that never came). |

Gradient and side alternate (teal→orange on the left / orange→teal on the right) so the three read as different in Test & Compare.

## C. Petrismo vs Abelardo's beret (13 Nov 2026)
Title: "El petrismo ataca la BOINA de Abelardo y calla sobre Mancuso" → thumbnail of Petro with **¿Y SUS BANDERAS?**
Why: the title already names the beret and Mancuso. The thumbnail opens the new, unmentioned angle (the M-19 flags), which is also the new topic of this video. Mancuso and the stage in Medellín had already been used in an earlier video.

## Anti-examples (what not to do)
- Title "Petro dejó perder los Panamericanos" + thumbnail "PETRO PERDIÓ LOS JUEGOS" → repeats the title, no new tension.
- "CORRUPTO" over the face of someone who hasn't been convicted → legal risk. Use "¿?" or point at the fact.
- Two faces small side by side, plus a logo, a date and three lines of text → fails the feed and the grayscale test.
- A thin or script font, or yellow text over a light orange area → unreadable at phone size.
