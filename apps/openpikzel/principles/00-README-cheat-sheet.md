## The one-screen version
1. **The thumbnail never repeats the title.** The title says *what*. The thumbnail adds tension or opens a *new question*. (Title "Petro dejó perder los Panamericanos…" → thumbnail "¿QUIÉN NO PAGÓ?", not "PETRO PERDIÓ".)
2. **One idea, one focal point, 2-3 elements max:** subject + text + one optional supporting object, kept faint.
3. **Text: 2-4 words, ALL CAPS, two lines.** Line 1 in yellow (#FFD700), line 2 in white, bold condensed sans with a thick black outline. Red only on ¿ and ?.
4. **A real face with a readable emotion,** large, cut out, rim-lit, on one third of the frame. The text goes in the open space on the opposite side.
5. **House look:** documentary style, teal↔orange gradient (#008080 / #FF6600), strong vignette, harsh side light, light grain, red play button top-left, no date, no logo, no extra text.
6. **Pull ONE lever:** MIEDO, INTRIGA or DESEO. Name it before you write.
7. **Cover test:** the thumbnail must work with no title, and the title with no thumbnail.
8. **Legal:** ESTAFA / MINTIERON / ROBO about a named person only if the script backs it. Otherwise turn it into a question ("¿?"), attribute it, or point at the fact rather than the person.
9. **Never promise what the video doesn't deliver.** The first 5 seconds of the video must confirm the thumbnail's promise.
10. **Prompt in English, image text in Spanish.** Spell the image text exactly, with accents and ¿¡.
11. **Three thumbnails per video,** one per title (main + 2 alternates). Each makes a *different* promise, not a rewording.
12. **Grayscale + mobile test:** shrink it to phone size and convert to grey. The face and the text must still win.
