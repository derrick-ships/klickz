# 07 · Repackaging a stalled video

## When to act
Only if impressions are still rising or holding steady **and** retention is above the channel average. In that case the video is fine and the packaging is the problem. If retention is weak, a new thumbnail won't save it.

Noticias BQ baseline (28 days, 11 Oct to 7 Nov 2026): 8.6% CTR, average view duration 2:13.

## Ladder (one change at a time, wait 24-48 h between steps)
1. **Sharpen the thumbnail:** 2-4 words, a new tension, passes the cover test.
2. Swap in an alternate title from "Títulos alternos".
3. Update the wording (year, names, current framing).
4. Tie it to a current Colombian news event, if the script supports it. This is the strongest lever for this channel.
5. Last resort: remake the video bigger.

## How to change a thumbnail
- First, diagnose in one line which lever the current one pulls and why it isn't getting clicks.
- **Change the lever and/or the format, not just the words.** Example: ¿INTOCABLE? (vague intrigue about the person) → NO SON LOS JUECES (intrigue that points at a hidden mechanism, a new question: then who is it?).
- Try the two reframes:
  - A **proven format:** ANTES / DESPUÉS, DÍA 1 / DÍA [X], MITO / REALIDAD, REAL / FALSO, or a comparison of two objects.
  - A **question:** "¿QUÉ TAN PELIGROSO?", "¿QUIÉN…?". Avoid opening with "¿POR QUÉ…?".
- If you borrow from an outlier (from the channel or a neighbour), write in one line WHY it got clicks (lever, open question, character) and carry over the mechanism, never the words. If you can't explain why it worked, don't borrow from it.
- Every proposal to Derrick says: old lever/format → new lever/format, in one line. Derrick makes the change in Studio, then it's measured at 24-48 h.
